Connect Claude through your browser

A small extension reads your usage with the claude.ai sign-in you already have in the browser, and sends it only to this app. Set it up once per browser profile.

  1. In Chrome or Edge, open chrome://extensions (Edge: edge://extensions) and turn on Developer mode.
  2. Click Load unpacked and choose this folder:
  3. Make sure you are signed in at claude.ai in that browser. The account appears here within a minute.

For another Claude account, load the extension in the browser profile that is signed in to it.
