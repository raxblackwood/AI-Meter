Quick 3-step setup
  1. Download the zip file above and extract it, or click Open Folder.
  2. In Chrome or Edge, open chrome://extensions (Edge: edge://extensions) and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and select the unzipped extension folder.
